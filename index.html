Precision Bearing Solutions

Jai Ambe Industries manufactures specialized bearing assemblies engineered for demanding automotive and industrial applications.

Water Pump Bearing

A crucial element that substitutes the traditional water pump assembly with an integral shaft bearing, this component circulates coolant from the radiator throughout the engine, ensuring effective cooling.

  • Manufacturing range: 20 to 62 mm sleeve OD with 12 to 31 mm dia. shaft
  • Product design & concurrent engineering capability
  • Well‑equipped reliability lab for design validation & endurance testing of bearings (bearing types: BB, BR, 2/3/4‑point angular contact bearings)
  • Seals: double lip seals and special "K‑Type Seal", a patented design of RPAL
  • Special operation capability: cross hole, knurling, taper hole, flats, woodruff keyway, internal/external threads, slots, grooves, counter sunk holes etc.

Cage and Needle Roller Assemblies

Cage and roller assemblies are compact bearings that are easy to handle and provide a substantial load capacity despite their small dimensions. The distinctive cage structure guides the needle rollers and facilitates efficient lubrication, even during high‑speed operations.

Cage and roller assemblies feature controlled contour crowning to reduce stress concentrations on the roller end from moderate misalignment/shaft bending, thereby extending bearing life.

Features
  • User friendly
  • Easy‑to‑handle, with a unique cage structure that keeps the rollers in place
  • Easy‑to‑lubricate – the outside surface of the cage forms the guide surface, providing good lubrication conditions
Applications
  • Planetary gears in automobiles
  • Constant mesh gearboxes
  • Industrial machinery
  • Robot speed reducers
